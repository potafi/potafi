### Hi, I'm Filippo 👋

**PhD in Physical Chemistry · R&D Scientist in industrial / green chemistry**

I work at the intersection of **chemistry, waste valorisation, and process scale-up** turning fundamental catalytic and separation science into real industrial processes for a more circular, lower-carbon chemical industry.

🔬 **PhD at Trinity College Dublin**: designed Metal@Carbon:Nitrogen materials for the electrocatalytic hydrogenation of biomass-derived organics: an electron-driven alternative to conventional thermal / H₂ routes.

🏭 **Now in R&D**: catalysis, separation, and process development from bench to pilot scale in circular-economy chemistry.
